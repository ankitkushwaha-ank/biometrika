About Biometrika-Core

Building the future of human verification

Our Mission

In an era where AI agents and bots are becoming increasingly sophisticated, we believe the internet should remain a space for human interaction. Biometrika-Core was built to protect that future.

Traditional CAPTCHAs frustrate users and can be bypassed by modern AI. We take a different approach: analyzing natural human behavior patterns that are nearly impossible for bots to replicate. Our invisible verification happens in the background, letting real users browse freely while keeping automated threats at bay.

Our Technology

Our proprietary behavioral analysis engine monitors dozens of interaction signals—from mouse movement curves to typing rhythm variations. Machine learning models trained on millions of verified human sessions can distinguish natural behavior from programmed patterns with exceptional accuracy.

We're privacy-focused by design. No personal data is collected, and all analysis happens in real-time with no persistent storage of behavioral patterns.
